Everything above this was preparation you can do in a notes app on a phone. This is the step that needs the software, and the step that produces the thing you are actually making.

Do: the five moves (they're also on the card you keep on screen, the guide), every card, every scene.

  1. Type the slug from the card
  2. Enter late — first line = motion already happening
  3. Behavior, not feelings — action blocks ≤4 lines
  4. Nobody says exactly what they mean
  5. Exit early — end on the change, three lines before it feels finished

All three climactic scenes in full — all five moves visible:

"Backup":

INT. SERVER ROOM - NIGHT

Rows of dead machines. One laptop glows -- a sticker-covered
ThinkPad. NADIA (29, hoodie, a lanyard that isn't hers) stares
at the screen.

ON SCREEN: RESTORE FROM THIS DEVICE? ALL LOCAL FILES WILL BE
ERASED. Y/N

Her phone buzzes on the desk. Speaker on.

                    COO (V.O.)
          Nadia. It's four a.m. Tell me we
          have a backup.

                    NADIA
          We have a laptop.

She opens a folder: UNFINISHED. Forty files. The last one is
named after her.

                    COO (V.O.)
          Then use it.

Nadia takes off the lanyard. Sets it down gently, like
something sleeping.

She presses Y.

The progress bar crawls. She doesn't look away.

"The Survey":

INT. CALL CENTER - LATE NIGHT

Rows of cubicles, half-dark. MARCUS (30s, headset, a coffee
cup holding down a stack of completed-call forms) stares at
his screen. 67 of 100.

                    CLARA (V.O.)
          Can I ask you something?

Marcus's fingers hover over the keyboard.

                    MARCUS
          That's not... the survey goes the
          other way around.

                    CLARA (V.O.)
          I know. Do you actually like
          your job?

The supervisor's footsteps: a slow patrol two aisles over.

Marcus pulls off the headset for one beat. Just holds it.
Looks at the 67. Then puts it back.

                    MARCUS
          I used to have a different one.

A long pause. Highway noise on her end.

                    CLARA (V.O.)
          What happened to it?

The footsteps stop nearby.

Marcus clicks: CALL COMPLETE. Types his notes. Closes the form.

He doesn't answer. He can't, and they both know it.

"The Return":

INT. CHILDHOOD BEDROOM - AFTERNOON

Twelve years of absence in one room: yellowed posters, a twin
bed stripped to the mattress. ELI (40s, rental car keys still
in hand) moves along the wall.

He opens the closet. Stops.

On the inside of the door: a hand-drawn calendar. Hundreds of
small squares. Some empty. Some crossed through with pen.

He leans closer. The top reads: DAY 1.

                    GRACE (O.S.)
              (from downstairs)
          Your hour's almost up.

Eli doesn't move. He counts. Can't stop counting.

The squares go all the way to today.

He closes the closet door quietly. Sits down on the bare
mattress. Puts his face in his hands.

Not leaving. Not yet.

That's the entire craft. Everything else is repetitions.
