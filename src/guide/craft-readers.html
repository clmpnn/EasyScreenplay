Readers

When the draft is clean enough to survive being read · Ongoing

What to ask, who to ask, and what to do with what they say.

  1. Recruited swap partners — the backbone. Two active by the P2 gate (Nov 2026); a third by the time P5's reader rounds start (Feb 2027). Sources: r/Screenwriting · Stage 32 · ISA Networks · MOOC classmates · Script Revolution. Template message: "I'm building a body of short scripts and I want to get better faster. Want a standing monthly swap — your pages for mine, notes within a week, written only?"
  2. Etiquette: give first · specific, page-referenced notes · never argue a note in the moment.
  3. Public threads for micro-feedback: loglines · first ten pages (each draft).
  4. MOOC peer review. (Sundance Collab is no longer a free-tier option — its self-paced screenwriting course is $375. Don't plan on it as a free reader source → the guide.)
  5. Give notes monthly (2 scripts minimum) — diagnosing someone else's saggy Act 2 is your best craft class.
  6. Optional: ONE Black List evaluation on your flagship feature — only after it passes the the guide quality bar, only as calibration before the Nicholl. Real cost: $30/month hosting plus ~$100 per evaluation — budget ~$130 minimum (one month's hosting plus one evaluation), or ~$260–290 for two evaluations plus three months' hosting, which is the realistic figure if you're using it to calibrate before the Nicholl. A score of 8+ unlocks free additional hosting and evaluations, top-of-list placement and inclusion in industry emails. Need-based fee waivers exist.

Note the coupling: the Black List is also the mechanical requirement for the Nicholl's Black List portal — a script must be hosted and carry at least one pending or completed evaluation at the moment you opt in. If you plan to enter through that portal in June 2027, the hosting and evaluation are not optional extras; they're the entry fee. (The WGF portal charges a straight entry fee — $100 for the first 750 entries, $120 after — with no hosting or evaluation requirement. Cheaper, if the Nicholl is the only reason you were considering the Black List → the guide.)

The table read — twice in the campaign

One 60–90-min video call · 4–6 people · parts pre-assigned · you read no part — you take notes: where they lean in, where they check phones · record with consent · scheduled 3 weeks out, script sent 5 days prior.
