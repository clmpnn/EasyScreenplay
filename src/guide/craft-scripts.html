The 2025 platform collapse — confirmed, and worse than the old note said

All five are permanently gone. Every one was a Cast & Crew / Industry Arts property wound down together.

Platform Closed Note
WeScreenplay 31 Jan 2025 —
Launch Pad (The Tracking Board's competition) 31 Jan 2025 Operator's own notice
ScreenCraft 28 Feb 2025 No successor exists. Its functions dispersed
The Script Lab 28 Feb 2025 Domain no longer resolves
Coverfly 1 Aug 2025 Domain has no DNS record at all. Profiles went dark 1 Sep 2025; all contest accolades earned there were lost

The Tracking Board as a publication: domain doesn't resolve; no formal shutdown notice found. Treat as defunct.

Two live consequences. (1) Any "best screenwriting contests" list that still recommends ScreenCraft, WeScreenplay or The Script Lab is out of date — including the widely circulated Lauri Donahue list, last updated January 2023. Check the date on every list you read. (2) There is no longer a single platform that carries your placement history between contests. Keep your own tracker.
