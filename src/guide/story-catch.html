Catch an idea

≤5 min

Three sentences, five minutes. Quality is the next section’s job.

Do: any idea, three sentences: Situation. Person + want. Why not easy. Quality is Is it a story yet?'s job.

"Backup": Servers die the night before launch. Nadia wants the system restored by 9 a.m. The only backup is on her dead mentor's laptop — restoring it erases the last thing he ever made. "The Survey": A phone survey company calls the one person who answers with weaponized honesty. Marcus wants to log 100 calls by shift's end. She keeps exposing that every question on his script is a lie. "The Return": A man comes back to his hometown after twelve years. Eli wants one hour inside the house before it's sold. His sister is the listing agent — and hasn't spoken to him in twelve years.

Out of ideas? Five sparkers: a moment from your work outsiders wouldn't believe · a decision you almost made differently · two people who should never share a room — stuck in one · a rule that exists for a reason nobody remembers · something you've always wanted to ask someone but never did.
